## Selection and licensing

- **EFX tree cards.** The archive FBX matches the earlier audited copy by SHA-256. The release selects two 12-triangle cards, packs their cutouts into one 512 px WebP and remaps the UVs. The cards run only in the distant broadleaf layer. Close views from the earlier review exposed the card intersections. The verified publisher license is CC BY 4.0. [Earlier comparison](docs/visual-audit-4.4/MEASUREMENTS.md#supplied-trees-and-bush-pack) · [Publisher model and license](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/f2a25ee70df440c9ab03d57aba2dc3f2)
- **Nicholas-3D grasses.** The 794-triangle, five-material GLB matches the publisher listing. Two selected meshes share a 512 px atlas. They supplement the banks outside the lanes, use cutout rendering and omit the original normal maps. The wheatgrass subject serves as understory. The Midwest's mature corn uses an original stalk with curled leaves, tassels and ears. [Publisher model and license](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/9b958d613e9a44dbba580748e7a1789c)
- **Fab and Quixel.** The converter name and package metadata establish format or vendor clues. They do not establish the original download entitlement or project redistribution rights. Fab distinguishes incorporating an asset into a project from distributing the source on its own. Some historical Megascans acquisitions carry engine-specific terms. The unresolved files stayed outside the repository and the game package. [Fab terms](https://www.fab.com/eula?lang=en) · [Epic content terms](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/content)
- **FeralMotion.** The shipped Labrador keeps its tested movement, vest and low slide. The uploaded replacement contains 21 motion clips and four embedded 2K images. The older comparison documents retargeting and browser delivery limits. [Earlier Labrador comparison](docs/visual-audit-4.4/MEASUREMENTS.md#supplied-feralmotion-labrador) · [Publisher](https://feralmotion.itch.io/labrador)

The repository includes the selected files' [attribution and edits](src/assets/environment/ATTRIBUTION.md) and the full [CC BY 4.0 license](src/assets/environment/CC-BY-4.0.txt). Raw archives, duplicate tiers and the other source files stay outside the build.
