Follow a yellow Lab through the cattails, creek beds, and flooded timber.

Swipe or use arrows to steer, jump, and duck.

How to hunt

Run the Great Migration with your retriever. Keep to a clear lane and collect treats and marked birds along the trail.

← → Lanes
Swipe left or right, or use the arrow buttons.
↑ Jump
Swipe up to clear logs and narrow crossings.
↓ Slide
Swipe down to duck under low timber.
Retrieves
Line up with marked birds to pick them up. Wildlife off the trail goes in your Journal.

Hunts earn career XP and furnishings for the Lodge. Visit the Kennel to fit earned gear.
