# Retriever Run environment art attribution

**landscape-tree-cards.glb** and **landscape-tree-atlas.webp** use `tree2` and `TreeSmall` from *Trees and bush Pack LOWPOLY* by EFX (@evan4129, currently displayed as sixonetwo). The runtime export selects two silhouettes, normalizes scale and pivots, packs and resizes the two cutouts, remaps UVs and applies alpha testing. The source was supplied by the project owner. [Model and creator](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/trees-and-bush-pack-lowpoly-f2a25ee70df440c9ab03d57aba2dc3f2) · [Publisher license record](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/f2a25ee70df440c9ab03d57aba2dc3f2)

**landscape-grass.glb** and **landscape-grass-atlas.webp** use two meshes from *Realistic Grass Pack For Games Free!* by Nicholas-3D (@Nicholas01). The runtime export selects the low wheat grass and one grass clump, combines two resampled colors into one atlas, remaps UVs, and removes normals maps and extra material slots. The source was supplied by the project owner. [Model and creator](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-grass-pack-for-games-free-9b958d613e9a44dbba580748e7a1789c) · [Publisher license record](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/9b958d613e9a44dbba580748e7a1789c)
