# The Great Migration territory registry

The game compresses habitats into a northbound sporting journey inspired by the Mississippi and Central flyway regions. The sequence takes 450 seconds of active play at two 15-second stages per territory. Routes, distances, weather timing, buildings and the westward turn into the Rockies are authored game geography. Bird species have their own migration routes. The links below are references for habitat and landform only. No source page images or meshes are bundled.

Landmark coordinates are streamed sample positions. The habitat descriptions are registry entries intended for future encounter logic, and tile packet anchors are a representative shore and ground subset. Phase 04 should select the species by water depth, cover and territory, and can derive additional open-water and canopy anchor distributions without modifying gameplay lanes. Climate analogues for the parklands inform the aspen and grass mosaic. All structures are original interpretations, including the elevated coastal and swamp styles, rather than reconstructions of named places.
