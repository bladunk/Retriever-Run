# Phase 02 obstacle and movement reference

All live hazard IDs come from [the obstacle registry](../../src/obstacles.js), the [seeded director](../../src/runner.js), and the [shared collision solver](../../src/collision.js). A dodge means moving until the Lab is physically outside the object. Steering can overlap with either vertical action. A low, visible object still requires a well-timed jump. The game steps at 120 Hz while rendering at the selected cadence.

The table's approach targets measure seconds until the first possible contact. They are visual review targets rather than per-model guarantees. A timed hazard needs a recognizable route by **1.1 s** before contact, or about 32 m at the current top speed. A lane choice needs **0.9 s** or about 26 m. The terrain surfaces, obstacles and supports need to be checked from the runner camera in actual weather. The validator separately holds **0.42 s** for choosing an action, adds the measured movement setup and requires timed action clearance with **±0.050 s** of execution latitude.

## Debugging and reproducibility

Serve the actual built artifact at `/Retriever-Run/` on localhost. Add `?debug=collision&seed=7&at=174&scenery=on` for a reproducible opening approach. Use `volumes=off` for an unobstructed frame. Diagnostics show hull facets, dog volumes including water probes, inferred lane and target lane, action time, impact pose and contact object. The contact marker represents the contacted dog body part at the first sweep fraction. The last 16 accepted skill events are observable in the diagnostic session. The query is disabled on hosted production and uses isolated practice progress locally.

Report the exact seed, course ruleset, pattern identifier, distance, stage, territory, action and diagnostic contact part. Worn gear, quality and device refresh rate leave the physical contract unchanged. The Lab visual rig follows the same jump height and crouch blend. A future GSP rig should be fitted to that contract.

Skill events are emitted after clearance or the optional route's third retrieve, once per encounter. A successful geometric action still needs to avoid the standing-pose contact to earn jump/slide mastery. A right-lane run through the forked tree does not award a jump or slide. Career multipliers and new mastery achievements are later work.
