# Art direction and asset production

Retriever Run depicts North American waterfowl country from the eye level of a running retriever. Start with habitat, season and believable construction. Gulf Coast Marsh combines shallow tidal channels, a compact sand-and-silt access trail, wet-bank plants, sun-bleached driftwood and a raised working shelter. Draw its palette from weathered wood, muted amber daylight, brackish green water and marsh grasses. Reserve contrast in the active lanes for the dog, warning silhouettes and pickups.

| Subject | Direction | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terrain and water | Compact gravel toward the trail center, damp sand at the edge, thin clay ruts, bank debris and subdued depth-dependent highlights | Match trail center at a turn, first creek approach and silhouette distance. Check shallow-angle sampling for stripes, bright fog, clipping and repeats |
| Plants | Broad cattail blades and recognizable brown heads at shallow edges. Thin cordgrass grows in uneven clumps where height permits | Keep the sightline to mandatory obstacles open. Use opaque instancing and simplify offshore silhouettes |
| Structures | Raised decks, readable stairs, bracing, stained timbers and practical metal roofing | Check solid geometry, shore contact and appearance through the full camera pass |
| Wildlife | Species silhouette before markings. Use accurate colors, anatomically credible flight, restrained specularity and recognizable size at play distance | Check takeoff, flock spacing and visible duration in portrait. Imported rigs prepare in menus and safe breaks |
| Canines and equipment | Working Labrador proportions, believable muscle and paw placement, restrained coats and working field gear. Treat Boyd’s approved likeness as the mascot and separate prestige character | Validate bind-space accessory contact during running, jumping, sliding and carrying. Gameplay remains authoritative |

## Asset stages

1. Verify the asset's specific license and ownership. Lock downloadable inputs to the recorded SHA-256 and retain credits. Use built game derivatives, with sanitized intake outside Git for private references.
2. Author or clean in Blender at a known metre scale. Preserve rig contracts on existing characters, use shared materials and establish two mesh tiers for abundant species. Bake source colors into scan vertices where a separate material would dominate the cost of a small prop.
3. Export indexed glTF. Run Meshoptimizer simplification against a screen-space purpose, validate index bounds, then quantize render attributes. Keep editable author inputs, raw textures, packages and renders separate from the checked-in runtime assets. The terrain builder merges opaque props into its existing tiles.
4. Pack PBR textures by color space. Preserve normal XY and roughness without lossy color conversion. Wrap gutters within each cell and limit distant footprints to prevent cross-map seams. Use sRGB GPU sampling for color. Keep water reflection approximations in the existing pass.
5. Publish through the verified asset manifest. Assign the content group to the region. Confirm cold preparation, intact-file reuse, offline operation and safe activation. Release regional textures after the last visible region draw, separately from the installed cache.
6. Render before and after from the same seed, pose, distance, lighting and mobile viewport. Compare Battery Saver against higher detail. Inspect ordinary moving gameplay, retirement and replay before promoting artwork beyond the slice.

## Expansion beyond Gulf Coast

Apply the texture and instancing contracts to Cypress and flooded timber with dense roots and high-water lines. Delta, Midwest and Platte need distinct sediment, field residue and banks. Use grass scatter and locally authored outbuildings for Nebraska, then wetland-depth and frost treatments in the Dakotas and Canadian marshes. Boreal and mountains need shorter tree LODs, separate stone and snow material sets and matched native memory traces. Before expanding, approve the Gulf camera evidence and run the physical iPhone checks in the [Phase 05.2 report](PHASE_05_2_REPORT.md).
